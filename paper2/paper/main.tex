\begin{abstract}
We study anticanonical slope stability of tangent bundles for two
classes of smooth toric Fano varieties.
For codimension-two blowups of products of projective spaces indexed by
a tree, stability is equivalent to one divisor-degree inequality at
each vertex. The exceptional contributions are mixed intersections on
the blowup centres. Stability holds for every tree of maximum degree
at most three whose nonleaf factor dimensions equal their valences, with arbitrary
positive leaf dimensions. A specified degree-four neighbourhood forces
instability; other degree-four and degree-five arrangements are stable.
For locally trivial toric bundles with fibre a product of degree-six
del Pezzo surfaces and base a product of projective spaces, the largest
Picard number compatible with tangent stability in dimension $n\ge4$
is $\lfloor5n/4\rfloor+1$, allowing arbitrary integral twists.
Every fixed tree of maximum degree at most three with an edge yields bundles
attaining this bound after sufficiently long independent edge subdivisions.
Their number, up to isomorphism of toric fans, is unbounded as the
dimension grows through multiples of four. The proofs use relative
tangent bundles, ranks of subspaces spanned by rays, and integral
comparisons. The bundle existence proofs also use finite exact rational
calculations with uniform error estimates and induction.
\end{abstract}
